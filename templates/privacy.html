Privacy Policy

Last updated: March 2024

1. Data Collection

We collect information you provide directly to us, such as your username, email address, and profile picture. When you use our platform, we also collect data about your auctions and bids.

Your information is used to manage your account, facilitate auctions, and provide support. We may use your email to send notifications about your bidding activity.

3. Data Sharing

We do not sell your personal data. We only share information necessary to facilitate the auction process, such as showing your username to other bidders.

4. Security

We take reasonable measures to protect your personal information from unauthorized access, loss, or misuse.
