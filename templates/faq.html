Frequently Asked Questions

Everything you need to know about AuctionHub

How do I create an auction?
Once you're logged in, click the "＋ Create" button in the navbar. Fill in the item name, description, starting price, end time, and optionally upload an image. Your auction goes live immediately!
How does bidding work?
Navigate to any active auction and enter a bid amount higher than the current price. You can use the quick-bid buttons (+₹1, +₹5, +₹10, +₹50) for convenience. The highest bid at the end of the auction wins.
Can I edit or delete my auction?
Yes! You can edit the item name, description, end time, and image of your active auctions from My Auctions. You can delete an auction only if it has no bids yet.
What is the 6-month re-auction rule?
Once an item is sold through an auction, it cannot be re-auctioned for 180 days (6 months). This policy ensures marketplace integrity and prevents price manipulation.
